Our Story

Who We Are

VinciFoods is a local student-led project developed in Valenzuela, Philippines. We are dedicated to exploring the intersection of modern web design and efficient delivery logistics. As visual learners, we prioritize a UI that is both striking and functional, utilizing a "Cyber-Purple" aesthetic to redefine the digital dining experience.

The Vision

To showcase a seamless "Order-to-Checkout" flow using local storage systems and high-end animations inspired by modern interactive platforms.

Our Roots

Developed as a final report project at Our Lady of Fatima University, this demo integrates persistent cart management and responsive UI design.

Tech Stack

Built with HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript, emphasizing procedural logic and clean data management.
